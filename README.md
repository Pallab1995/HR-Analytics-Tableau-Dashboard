# 📊 **HR Analytics Dashboard — Employee Attrition & Workforce Insights (Tableau Project)**
**🔗 Live Dashboard:**  
https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/pallab.sharma/viz/Project2_17573688772370/Dashboard1 

This Tableau project provides an in-depth analysis of **employee attrition**, **workforce demographics**, **monthly income trends**, and **tenure distribution**.  
The dashboard helps HR teams identify **who is leaving**, **why they are leaving**, and **how to improve retention** using data-driven insights.

---

## 🔍 **Project Overview**

The main objectives of this HR Analytics dashboard are:

- Analyse **attrition patterns** across age groups, income levels, and job satisfaction  
- Evaluate **employee distribution** by income, age, and years at company  
- Understand **salary trends** across different age segments  
- Highlight important HR KPIs such as:  
  - **Employee Count**  
  - **Attrition Rate %**  
  - **Job Satisfaction Score**  

The dashboard is designed for HR teams to gain actionable insights for **employee engagement**, **workforce planning**, and **retention strategies**.

## 🧩 **Key Insights from the Dashboard**

- **Attrition is highest** in the **26–35 age group**  
- Lower monthly income groups show **higher attrition rates**  
- Employees with more years at the company tend to stay longer  
- Income increases steadily with age, improving retention  
- Most employees fall within the **1–5 years tenure range**  
- Job satisfaction score indicates opportunities for employee engagement improvements
